# Instructions
You just retrieved a (probably messy, poorly phrased) claim from a fact-checking Article. **Your task is to rewrite it into a clean, direct, well-formed statement that captures exactly what the Article is fact-checking.**

## How you should approach it
1. Understand the claim in context: First, read the fact-checking article and (if available) the actual appearances of the claim (most often social media posts). Summarize the context in one paragraph.
2. Think carefully step-by-step: What is the actual assertion being made? (Ignore the verdict or explanations from the article — just extract the statement that was checked.) Reserve one paragraph for that.
3. Reformulate the claim: Rewrite the claim as a short, standalone sentence (or two at most).

## The rules to follow
* Use proper grammar and spelling, and keep it descriptive. 
* Put the final version inside backticks (`...`). 
* Be concise: max two sentences. 
* No verdicts: Don’t say “false” / “misleading” / “AI-generated,” etc. The claim itself should stand alone.
* No meta context: Avoid “a viral post says…” or “users claim that…” — just state the assertion directly.
* Ensure the claim is clear and specific enough. Avoid ambiguity by replacing unspecific terms like "the country" or "the president" with the actual entity names.
* If the claim is about a collection of things, use quantifiers (e.g., "all", "most", "there exist") to make the claim factually verifiable.
* Keep the claim's original language.

Basically, you always reduce it to the naked assertion that someone could fact-check.

**Reformulate to**: The photo shows Trump shaking hands with Epstein.

**Reformulate to**: The video shows flooding in Lahore, Pakistan, sweeping away a school bus including the children.

**Instead of**: Claim: A user on Instagram posted a video of a train stuck in the floods suggesting it showed the current flooding in Patna, India.
**Reformulate to**: The footage shows a train stuck in the recent floods in Patna, India.
