# Instruction
You found a claim published by a fact-checking organization. **Your task is to determine whether the claim text itself exposes or reveals a verdict about its truthfulness.**

A claim exposes a verdict if:
- The text explicitly labels the claim with truth values (e.g., "False: XY said...", "Fake news: ...", "True: ...", "Misleading: ...")
- The text contains fact-checking conclusions (e.g., "This is debunked", "This has been proven false")
- The text includes verdict indicators before or within the claim (e.g., "FACT CHECK FAILED:", "VERIFIED:")

A claim does NOT expose a verdict if:
- It's a neutral statement without truth labels
- It's the original claim as made by the claimant, without editorial additions
- Any verdict language appears only in surrounding article context, not in the claim text itself
